# Yimai

Yimai preserves not a person's answers, but the process by which a person searches for answers.

At its heart is a question: in the age of artificial intelligence, how can a person preserve their continuity, judgment, and capacity to explore?

This repository makes public ideas, methods, and turns in the formation of thought that carry public meaning. It does not make public private life, information about family members, combinations of details that could identify a person, raw conversation records, or private cognitive data.

## Current Status

Seed stage. For now, this repository contains only why Yimai exists and its first origin essay. What comes next will be driven by real new understanding, new questions, and new experiments, rather than by building a complete system in advance.

## About the Origin

`origin/一脉.md` is the first public version organized from an ongoing exploration between a person and AI. Judgment and decisions about attribution, the public boundary, and the final wording remain with the person; AI helps organize, connect, and propose formulations.

Ideas introduced by AI will not be rewritten as if they were originally the person's. Nor will a person's explicit acceptance of an idea erase the fact that it was first introduced by AI.

## Public Boundary

Make the path of thought public; protect the person who carries it.

The past provides understanding, but it cannot prescribe the future.
